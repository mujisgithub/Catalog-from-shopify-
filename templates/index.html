Questions

Good to know

Which Shopify export do I need?

The standard product export: Shopify admin → Products → Export → “Plain CSV file”. Exporting all products or just a filtered view both work.

What ends up on each card?

The product photo, title, SKU and a price badge — and, if you add your store URL, a link straight to the product. Discount versions show the original price crossed out.

How do category sections work?

Type the category names you want (e.g. Electronics, Kitchenware). Products tagged with that name in Shopify go into that chapter, each with its own divider page. Anything untagged lands in a final “More Products” chapter.

Why don't stock counts show on my catalog?

Stock only appears when your export includes inventory numbers. In Shopify that means “Track quantity” must be switched on for those products.

Is my data stored?

Only long enough to build your PDF. Uploads and finished files are deleted automatically within two hours. We never sell or share your product data.

I paid — how do I use Pro?

After checkout you're sent back here with your license already filled in and saved in your browser. Using a different device? Email us and we'll help.
